Unlatched flasher

Put the firmware on a Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 straight from this page, with nothing to install.

This browser can't talk to USB serial devices

Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151 or newer, on a desktop or laptop. Phones and Safari can't flash a board from a web page.

Or flash from a terminal with esptool, as shown further down.

Open this page over HTTPS

Browsers allow serial access only from HTTPS pages or localhost.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox 151+ on desktop.

Before you start

  1. Plug the XIAO into this computer with a data USB-C cable. A charge-only cable is the usual reason a board doesn't show up.
  2. Click Connect & install and pick the board from the list. It's usually named USB JTAG/serial debug unit.
  3. If it isn't listed, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then try again.

Leave “Erase device” unticked when updating

Erasing wipes everything the rig has stored: its node address, the keys it shares with your windows, and your Wi-Fi and MQTT settings. A rig that's already paired would need pairing again.

Tick it only for a board that has never run Unlatched, or one that was running something else.

After flashing

  1. The board restarts and opens a Wi-Fi network named velux-rig-setup.
  2. Join it from your phone and choose your home Wi-Fi on the page that opens.
  3. Carry on from Joining your Wi-Fi in the guide.

A rig that's already on your network can be updated over the air instead. Use firmware.bin from the latest release.

Flashing from a terminal instead

Download firmware.factory.bin (the same image this page installs) and write it at offset 0:

esptool.py --chip esp32c6 --port /dev/cu.usbmodem101 write_flash 0x0 firmware.factory.bin
